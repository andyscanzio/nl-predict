# The Elo Model's Starting Ratings are carried over from past Seasons, in a committed file regenerated once per Season

Every team used to start the Season level at 1500, although last Season's table says a lot about this one. The Elo Model now starts each team from its Rating at the end of the previous Season, carried in full through every 14-team Season since 2022/23, and a promoted team takes over the Rating of the team it replaced. The Starting Ratings live in `data/starting-ratings.json`, written by `npm run starting-ratings` from the Season snapshots in the gitignored `data/local/seasons/`, and are regenerated by hand once per Season.

The Starting Rating study (`npm run study:starting-rating`) chose this. It ran walk-forward folds with 2023/24, 2024/25 and 2025/26 as test Seasons, each tuned on the Seasons before it only. Against a level start, the carry-over improved pooled per-Game Brier by 0.8%, better in 3 of 3 test Seasons, and pooled Rank RPS by 3.3% (with Rating Uncertainty, ADR 0005), better in 2 of 3. That met the agreed rule: better on both pooled, and on both in at least 2 test Seasons. The Rank RPS gain is within noise (95% interval −0.0100 to +0.0050), while the per-Game Brier gain is not. Tuned on 2023/24 to 2025/26, K fell from 15 to 10, Home Advantage from 70 to 60, and the carry-over share came out at 1.0, keeping all of last Season's spread.

## Considered Options

- **Keep the level start**: rejected. It predicts Games worse all Season, most of all early on.
- **Regress toward 1500 by a tuned share r**: kept as the general form (`startingRatingsFrom`). Tuned per fold, r came out at 0.6 to 1.0, and at 1.0 on all three Seasons.
- **Compute the Starting Ratings at build time from past Seasons**: rejected. The past Seasons' snapshots stay out of the repo (`data/local/` is gitignored), and they change only once a year.
- **Include 2021/22**: rejected. It had 13 teams, so Kloten's promotion would have had no relegated team to take over from.

## Consequences

- The file belongs to one Season. A test fails until it is regenerated for the Season in `data/games.json`, which blocks deploys at the Season rollover until someone runs `npm run starting-ratings`.
- A Back-Test of any other Season (`npm run backtest -- --in …`, the studies) uses a level-start Elo Model with the same K, Home Advantage and Rating Uncertainty (`eloModelFor`).
- The Elo Model's whole Projection History changed when this shipped (ADR 0003). Its first point now differs by team.
- The build's history cache key includes the file.
